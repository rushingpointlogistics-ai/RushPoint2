Frequently Asked Questions

How does RushPoint protect my money?
All order payments are held in automated 4-way escrow. Funds are only transferred to the vendor and rider after the delivery is completed with your unique 4-digit PIN.
Can I order from multiple vendors in one delivery?
Yes! You can add products from various verified stores to your cart. RushPoint calculates the optimal courier route and combined delivery fee automatically.
How do I download the mobile app on my phone?
You can download the official RushPoint app on the Google Play Store for Android or the Apple App Store for iPhone. You can also use the high-speed Web App directly from any browser.
