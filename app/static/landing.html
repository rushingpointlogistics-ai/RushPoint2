How RushPoint Works

From browsing your favourite physical stall to receiving your package at the door — fast, fair, and fully tracked.

STEP 01
🛍

Browse & Add to Cart

Explore verified physical merchant stalls — groceries, bakeries, hardware, and more. Add items to your cart with one tap.

STEP 02
📍

Your GPS Sets the Fee

The app reads your GPS or manual address. Real OSRM road distance between vendor stall and your location calculates the exact delivery fee — no flat rate surprises.

STEP 03
💰

Pay Your Way

Complete checkout via Bank Transfer, Debit Card, USSD code, QR Code, or RushPoint wallet. Funds enter secure escrow instantly via Flutterwave.

STEP 04
🚕

Rider Delivers, You Confirm

Assigned rider arrives at your location. You hand them your 4-digit delivery PIN to confirm receipt and release escrow funds automatically.

Transparent Pricing

How Delivery Fees Are Calculated

RushPoint uses real road distance — not flat guesswork. The admin sets the per-kilometre rate, and you pay exactly what the road costs.

🗺

Road Distance Base Fee

OSRM calculates actual driving distance from vendor stall to your GPS location. You pay by the kilometre.

📦

Multi-Item Surcharge (+0.2% per item)

If your order has more than 1 item from the same shop, a small 0.2% per-item surcharge is added on top of the base delivery fee.

📊

Bulk Order Fee (+4% when qty > 5)

Orders with more than 5 total products add a 4% fee of the base delivery charge to cover increased handling weight.

🚕

Vehicle Multiplier

Motorcycle = 1.0× (standard). Tricycle/Keke for bulky cargo = 1.6× (larger vehicle surcharge).

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about shopping, delivery fees, escrow security, and payments.

How does the road distance delivery fee work? ▼
RushPoint calculates your delivery fee using live OSRM road kilometres directly from the merchant's physical stall to your GPS coordinates. If you purchase multiple items from the same store, only a small +0.2% fee per extra item is added, and orders with over 5 products receive a +4% handling surcharge. No flat-rate guesswork!
Is my payment safe if a rider doesn't show up? ▼
Yes, 100%. Every purchase is protected by 4-digit Delivery PIN Escrow. Your payment is held safely in escrow and is only released when you physically inspect your parcel and hand your unique 4-digit PIN to the courier. If an order fails, funds remain in escrow and are refunded directly to your wallet.
What payment methods can I use to pay or top up? ▼
RushPoint supports multi-channel Flutterwave payments: direct Bank Transfer (every customer gets a unique dedicated Wema Bank virtual account), Debit Cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), USSD codes, QR Code, and direct RushPoint Wallet balance.
How do merchants join and receive their payouts? ▼
Merchants can register via the Partner Onboarding link. Once verified, stall owners receive 100% of their assigned product price with zero commission deductions. Sale revenues are credited to the merchant's wallet immediately when an order is placed.
How much do riders earn per delivery? ▼
Couriers receive an industry-leading 80% share of the total delivery fee for every completed mission. The moment the delivery PIN is confirmed on the courier's phone, earnings are deposited directly into their wallet for immediate withdrawal.
